Jilo Support

Jilo is a calm companion for lonely or heavy days. If something isn't working, or you have a question, we're happy to help.

If you are in danger right now, call your local emergency number. In the UK, call 999, or Samaritans free on 116 123, any time. In other countries, findahelpline.com lists free helplines. Inside Jilo, tap Get help: it is always free.

Common questions

How do I restore Jilo Plus on a new phone?

Open Jilo, go to Self-care, scroll down to Settings, and tap Restore purchase. On an iPhone, use the same Apple Account you subscribed with. On Android, use the same Google account.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone: open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Jilo Plus, and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

Android: open the Google Play app, tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, then Jilo Plus, and tap Cancel subscription. In Jilo, Manage in Settings takes you there too.

You keep Jilo Plus until the end of the month you paid for.

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. Jilo has no account and no server that receives what you write. To keep a copy, go to Self-care, Settings, and tap Save a backup. Use Restore to bring it back.

I forgot my PIN

Because everything stays private on your phone, a PIN can't be recovered. On the PIN screen, tap Forgot your PIN? to start fresh. This deletes what is in Jilo on that phone, so keep a backup.

Is Jilo a therapist?

No. Jilo is a wellbeing companion, not a medical service, and it doesn't give medical advice. If you are struggling, please talk to someone you trust, your doctor, or a helpline.
