Jilo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Jilo is a calm companion for lonely or heavy days. It is built to keep what you share private: everything you write in Jilo stays on your own phone.

What stays on your phone

Your check-ins, small things, notes, letters, worries, your safety plan, your good moments and their photos, your heart and oasis, and your chats with Jilo are stored only on your device. Jilo has no account and no server that receives them. Talk replies in the app are made on your phone. If you delete Jilo, this data is deleted with it, unless you saved a backup yourself.

Subscriptions

Jilo Plus is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles your payment, and we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID that is not linked to your name or email, your purchase and subscription history, and basic device information such as your device model, operating system version, app version and country. This is used only to give you access to what you paid for. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Apple's privacy policy.

Fonts

Jilo loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When it does, Google receives your IP address, as with any website. See Google's privacy policy.

Helplines and links

When you tap Call or Text next to a helpline, your phone's own Phone or Messages app opens. Jilo doesn't record who you contact.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics on what you write or how you feel.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.

Jilo is not a medical service

Jilo is not a therapist and does not give medical advice. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. For questions, see Jilo Support.
